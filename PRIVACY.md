# Privacy Policy

**Multi-Product Comparator** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## What data is stored

When you add a product to your compare list, the extension stores the following in your browser's session storage:

- Product name
- Product page URL
- List of shop names on that product page

This data exists only in your browser and is automatically cleared when you close the browser.

## What data is NOT collected

- No personal information
- No browsing history
- No analytics or tracking
- No cookies

## External requests

The extension makes no network requests. All data stays local to your browser.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/twttr/multi-product-comparator/issues.
